    cons:
      - Fn on this knob does nothing new
  - id: nudge
    title: One frame per click
    summary: With Fn held, each click moves the playhead one frame, to fine-tune right after a jump.
    pros:
      - Jump, then fine-position, on one knob
      - Matches "Fn means finer" on the scrub and zoom knobs
    cons:
      - Repeats what Fn + the scrub knob already does
      - Holding Fn by accident creeps a frame instead of jumping
  - id: picture
    title: Skip sound cuts
    summary: With Fn held, the knob skips sound strips and stops only at the starts and ends of strips that aren't sound.
    pros:
      - Music and sound-effect edits don't get in the way
      - Stops only where the picture changes
    cons:
      - A cut only in the sound is skipped
      - Two jump rules to remember

## Context

The new knob jumps the playhead to the next cut (click right) or the previous one (click left), on any channel. Until you decide, Fn + the knob does the same jump.
