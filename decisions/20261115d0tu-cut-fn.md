options:
  - id: same
    title: The same cut
    summary: Fn makes no difference; the button always cuts the same way.
    pros:
      - Nothing to remember
      - Fn stays free for something else later
    cons:
      - Only one kind of cut on the device
  - id: other-scope
    title: Cut the other set of strips
    summary: >-
      If the plain press cuts everything, Fn + press cuts only selected strips; otherwise
      Fn + press cuts everything under the playhead.
    pros:
      - Both scopes without the keyboard
    cons:
      - Easy to forget which is which
  - id: other-kind
    title: The other kind of cut
    summary: >-
      If the plain press makes a soft cut (K), Fn + press makes a hard one (Shift+K), and the
      other way round.
    pros:
      - Mirrors K and Shift+K
    cons:
      - The two kinds look the same on the timeline
