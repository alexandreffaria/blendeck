recommendation:
  option: all
  why: >-
    If you mostly cut every track at once (video, its audio, titles) to trim or drop a
    section, all saves selecting first; pick selected if you often cut one track and leave
    the others whole.
options:
  - id: all
    title: Everything under the playhead
    summary: >-
      Every strip the playhead crosses is cut, selected or not (locked strips and locked
      channels are skipped).
    pros:
      - No selecting first; titles and music on their own tracks are cut too
    cons:
      - A strip you want whole (a music bed) is cut too, unless its channel is locked
  - id: selected
    title: Only selected strips
    summary: Only selected strips under the playhead are cut, like Blender's K.
    pros:
      - Same as K
      - Lets you cut some tracks and leave the rest
    cons:
      - Selecting first needs the mouse or keyboard
      - Nothing happens if nothing is selected
  - id: selected-else-all
    title: Selected strips, or all if none are selected
    summary: >-
      With strips selected, only those are cut; with nothing selected, everything under
      the playhead is.
    pros:
      - Both uses from one button
    cons:
      - A forgotten selection cuts less than you expect
resolution:
  choice: selected-else-all
  resolved_at: 2026-11-15T06:16:17Z
---

## Context
