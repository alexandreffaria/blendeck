    title: Fn zooms faster

      - Gentle plain clicks for small changes
      - Already the default
    cons:
      - Opposite of the scrub knob, where Fn means finer

  - id: fine
    title: Fn zooms finer
    summary: A plain click zooms 4 steps (about 27%); with Fn, 1 step (7.5%), like the scrub knob's 10 and 1.
    pros:
      - Same meaning as on the scrub knob
      - Crosses a long edit without holding Fn
    cons:
      - Plain clicks move 27%; small adjustments need Fn
  - id: same
    title: Fn does nothing different
    summary: One step size with or without Fn (1 step by default, editable on the page).
    pros:
      - Nothing to remember
      - Fn stays free for something else later
    cons:
      - "One speed for everything: at 1 step, about 61 clicks from 5 s to 10 min"
  - id: fit
    title: Fn shows the whole edit
    summary: Turning either direction with Fn held fits all strips in view; plain clicks zoom 1 step.
    pros:
      - Back to the full edit without reaching for the keyboard
    cons:
      - No second zoom speed on the knob

## Context

On the scrub knob, Fn makes it fine (1 frame instead of 10). Fast is the default now;
fast, fine and same are just step settings (1 to 20 per binding), while fit needs add-on work.
