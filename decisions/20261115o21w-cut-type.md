recommendation:
  option: soft
  why: Soft lets you fix a cut made a few frames off by dragging the edge; pick hard only if you never want to drag a cut edge past where you cut.
options:
  - id: soft
    title: Soft cut (K)
    summary: Each half keeps the footage beyond the cut, so dragging its edge brings it back.
    pros:
      - Mistakes are fixed by dragging an edge
      - What Blender's K does
    cons:
      - An edge dragged too far shows footage you cut away
  - id: hard
    title: Hard cut (Hold Split, Shift+K)
    summary: Each half ends at the cut; its edge can't be dragged past it.
    pros:
      - Dragging an edge never brings back footage you cut away
    cons:
      - A cut a few frames off needs undo or the Sidebar's Hold Offset
resolution:
  choice: soft
  resolved_at: 2026-11-15T06:16:43Z
applied_at: 2026-11-15T07:47:35Z

## Context
